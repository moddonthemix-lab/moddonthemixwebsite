Engineering the artist’s vision.

Modd, professionally known as Moddonthemix, is a recording, mixing and mastering engineer based in Tampa Bay, Florida. His musical foundation began in band and chorus, where an interest in performance developed into a fascination with the technical side of sound.

Over seven years, he has developed his craft through studio work spanning Tampa Bay, Miami, Portland, Atlanta and New York. His approach combines analog recording techniques and modern digital workflows, with particular attention to vocal presence, dynamics, depth and the emotional intent of each record.

His professional portfolio includes work associated with artists such as iLoveMemphis, Coco Bliss and Mia Martina. Individual release credits and roles are available through his public discography and Muso.AI profile.

Today, Modd records at Summit Audio Recordings in Largo and provides remote mixing and mastering to independent artists worldwide. His goal is to deliver a record that reflects the artist’s identity, translates across playback systems and is prepared for release.
